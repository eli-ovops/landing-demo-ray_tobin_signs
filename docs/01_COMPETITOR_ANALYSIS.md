## Applied mapping

- **Visual language:** quiet workshop/editorial quality rather than copycat cartoon or competitor styling.
- **Layout:** one full-bleed hero, proof row, three services, short process, one contact route.
- **Conversion:** `tel:` and `mailto:` only; no self-built enquiry system.
- **Originality:** all prose is newly written; hero asset is AI-generated and marked Concept imagery.
